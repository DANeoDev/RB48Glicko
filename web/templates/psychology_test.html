1. Scope of the Rating System (What Glicko Is & Isn't)

RB48 uses an adapted Glicko-2 rating system. Its sole mathematical purpose is to summarize historical match results and lineup compositions to calculate expected win probabilities and help the Matchmaker generate competitive, balanced teams.

Important: Glicko is a result-based performance model. It does not measure individual physical stamina, technical flair, defensive work rate, leadership, or personal worth. The predictive power of the model is mathematically validated and continuously monitored under Model Analysis.

2. Why is Access Gated? (The Psychology of Rated Games)

In competitive gaming and recreational sports, introducing numerical ratings frequently triggers a well-documented psychological effect: the deterioration of intrinsic motivation.

When players fixate on external rating points rather than the pure joy of playing football with friends, harmless recreational sessions can morph into post-match blame games, rating anxiety, and locker-room toxicity.

Our Community Standard: We gate access behind this psychological evaluation to ensure all Glicko-tier members understand variance, respect sportsmanship, and prioritize the fun and camaraderie of Wednesday night football over a number.

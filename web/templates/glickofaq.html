Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RB48 matchdays, matchmaking, and performance statistics.

What is RB48?

RB48 is a private recreational football community that organizes weekly competitive small-sided matches. We focus on fair play, fun sportsmanship, balanced teams, and good banter after every session.

What is the difference between BOX and HF pitch formats?

We play on two distinct formats with different dynamics and team sizes:

  • BOX (Soccerbox): Fast-paced indoor/caged pitch. Standard playtime is 20:00 at Soccerbox - Uni Sport with a maximum capacity of 12 players (5v5)+2. Rebounds off walls and intense short-burst transitions.
  • HF (Half-Field): Outdoor half-pitch. Standard playtime is 20:30 at Halbfeld - Zülpicher Wall 5 with a maximum capacity of 18 players (8v8)+2. More open space, longer passing, and tactical positioning.

How can I participate as a Guest Player?

Visitors can join matchdays through the Attendance Planner.

To give registered club members first priority, guest player sign-ups unlock on the Sunday at 00:00 immediately preceding that matchday. Simply click "＋ Attend as Guest Player", enter your name, and you'll be placed on the roster or waiting list.

What equipment and footwear should I bring?

We recommend standard cleats, multicam shoes can work too, but especially the Box pitch can get very slippery if wet. The club provides a ball and vests.

How does the Attendance Planner and Waiting List work?

Members can RSVP (Attend or Decline) for any future scheduled match at any time in the Attendance Planner.

Sign-ups are strictly ordered by registration timestamp. When the maximum capacity is reached (12 for BOX, 18 for HF), subsequent sign-ups are placed on the Waiting List. If an active attendee cancels or declines, the #1 waiting list player is automatically promoted to the active lineup.

Can I bring friends or guest players as a member?

Yes! Members can register guest players directly to the matchday. Guest registration opens on the Sunday at 00:00 before the match. Guests added by members are automatically tagged with a linked suffix (e.g. Max (Konsti +1)) so everyone knows who invited them.

How does the Matchmaker create balanced teams?

Before kickoff, the staff uses the Match Center matchmaker. It evaluates the participating players and iterates through thousands of possible team permutations to minimize predicted win-probability skew, ensuring competitive, evenly matched games.

How do I link my account to my player profile and upload a photo?

Head to Profile Settings. You can upload a profile picture, configure your custom Attendance Display Name, and select your player alias. The Webmaster reviews and approves player link requests to keep the database authentic.

What is a Glicko-2 rating?

A Glicko-2 rating is a mathematical model that tracks historical match outcomes and team compositions to estimate how likely a player or team is to win future matches against given opponents.

Crucial Scope Distinction: Glicko is a result-based statistical model, not a measurement of personal skill, work rate, or human worth. A higher rating simply means historical match results involving that player have been favorable relative to the ratings of teammates and opponents.

What is RD (Rating Deviation) and why does it matter?

RD represents statistical uncertainty. A rating with a low RD (e.g., 1600 with RD 35) is well-established from frequent recent matches. A rating with high RD (e.g., 1600 with RD 120) means the model has less recent data.

When a player misses multiple matchdays, their RD naturally increases over time to reflect growing uncertainty about their current form.

What is the Conservative Rating used on the leaderboard?

To avoid ranking players who have played only one lucky match above consistent veterans, RB48 ranks the leaderboard by Conservative Rating:

Conservative Rating = Rating − (3 × RD)

This represents the statistical 99.7% lower-bound confidence estimate of a player's rating. Active players with established ratings and low RD are rewarded for consistency.

Why do a player's BOX and HF ratings diverge?

BOX (small-sided, wall rebounds, high-frequency shooting) and HF (open-field, tactical endurance, spatial awareness) require different physical and tactical skillsets. Some players excel at wall-play and rapid combinations, while others dominate in spacious 8v8 setups. Separate rating calculations allow us to model each pitch environment accurately.

How is the predictive accuracy of the model evaluated?

Under Model Analysis, we continuously track the Brier score, log-loss, and calibration curve of all historical match predictions. If the model predicts a 65% win probability across 100 matches, the favored team should win approximately 65 of those matches.

About RB 48 Köln e.V.

A private football association founded on the principles of fair competition, mathematical matchmaking, and genuine sportsmanship.

⚽ The Club & Community

RB 48 Köln e.V. is a recreational football association based in Cologne, Germany. We organize weekly small-sided matches for players who love high-tempo football, friendly competition, and sharing a cold drink and good banter after the final whistle.

What makes RB 48 special is our commitment to a positive locker-room culture. We play hard, we celebrate incredible goals and nutmegs, and we treat every teammate and opponent with mutual respect regardless of scoreline or skill level.

🏟️ Our Matchday Formats

We host regular sessions across two distinct pitch formats to challenge different tactical and physical skillsets:

Soccerbox (BOX)

Location: Soccerbox - Uni Sport
Schedule: Wednesdays @ 20:00
Format: 6v6 (12 Players Max)

High-intensity indoor/caged football with wall rebounds, rapid transitions, and quick one-touch combinations.

Halbfeld (HF)

Location: Halbfeld - Zülpicher Wall 5
Schedule: Wednesdays @ 20:30
Format: 9v9 (18 Players Max)

Spacious outdoor half-pitch with room for tactical positioning, long balls, stamina, and expansive team defense.

📐 Math for Fairness, Joy for Football

Have you ever played in a recreational game where one side completely dominated, making the match boring for both teams? We built an adapted Glicko-2 statistical rating model and automated Matchmaker to solve that problem.

By modeling historical lineup combinations and match outcomes, our matchmaking algorithm evaluates thousands of team permutations before kickoff to create the most evenly balanced squads possible.

Our Golden Rule: The mathematical model exists exclusively to make matches exciting and balanced. Ratings are result-based summaries of past games — they will never define individual character or human worth.
